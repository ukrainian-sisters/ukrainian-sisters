Who We Are

A movement built by solo mothers,
for solo mothers

Founded in December 2023, Ukrainian Sisters emerged from the front lines of the war's social crisis — a team of solo mothers and military wives who know first-hand what it means to hold everything together alone.

Our Mission

To unite the world around the support of solo motherhood on the path of life, increasing strength and impact on society.

Our Vision

A world where a solo mother has the freedom to choose her best version of life — independent, financially capable, fulfilled, protected and authentic.

Our Programs

Six pillars of
support & empowerment

01

Crisis Response & Social Support

Humanitarian aid, emergency shelter, legal consultations, case management and referral navigation for solo mothers in acute crisis situations.

02

Mental Health & Emotional Resilience

Individual and group therapy, art therapy, self-help programs, and psychological support groups for mothers and their children dealing with war trauma.

03

Culture, Creativity & Women's Identity

Art workshops, photography projects, cultural events and the "Voices of Solo Mothers" podcast — tools for authentic self-expression and Ukrainian identity.

04

Employment & Women's Entrepreneurship

Career counseling, business incubator for mothers, reskilling courses (SMM, IT, marketing), mentorship programs, and the "Solo Start-Up" initiative.

05

Advocacy for Women's Rights

Fighting stigma, advancing state recognition of solo motherhood, roundtables with authorities, and campaigns for gender equality and solo-family dignity.

06

Children's Support & Development

Festive events, art therapy, child-friendly spaces at hubs, educational programs and dedicated initiatives like "Super Call" and "Upside Down Day".

Who We Serve

Solo mothers in all their complexity

For us, "solo mothers" are women who de-jure or de-facto independently raise and provide for their children — especially those in temporary crisis or difficult life circumstances.

Single & Unmarried Divorced Widows Military wives War bereaved mothers IDP mothers Mothers of children with disabilities Refugee mothers Pregnant women without support Solo guardians

The scale of the crisis

Full-scale war has radically changed life for Ukrainian women with children. The vulnerability of this group has grown dramatically.

Ukraine's single-parent household poverty rate rose from 39% in 2021 to 67.9% in 2022, remaining above 65% through 2023.

6.7M Women need humanitarian assistance (UN Women, 2025)
2M+ Women with children have IDP status in Ukraine
34K Divorces registered in Ukraine in 2024 (+42% vs 2023)
15M Ukrainians may need psychological support (Ministry of Health)

Our Values

What drives everything we do

01 — Empowerment

Support & Empowerment

We stand beside solo mothers in difficult circumstances, helping them find inner strength, achieve their goals, and build confidence in their own future.

02 — Empathy

Empathy & Respect

We create a safe space built on trust, dignity and understanding. We listen to women's needs and respond with care and compassion.

03 — Authenticity

Authenticity

We value each woman's individuality. We encourage speaking one's truth — even when uncomfortable. Authenticity opens the path to self-discovery and confidence.

04 — Equality

Equality & Assertiveness

We believe in equal opportunities for all women and support their right to advocate for their own interests with respect for others.

05 — Resourcefulness

Resourcefulness

We provide tools, knowledge and opportunities that help solo mothers realize their potential and influence their own lives and society.

06 — Sisterhood

Sisterhood Synergy

We create a living community of women where mutual support and interaction flourish. Together we amplify each other, sharing experience and taking responsibility for change.

Our Story

From volunteers to
systemic change

February 2022

Born from the crisis

A volunteer group formed "Ukrainian Sisters" in the first weeks of the full-scale invasion. Within 4 months: 400+ solo mothers supported, humanitarian aid organized, and a community of 1,000+ members established. Mutual aid became our founding principle.

2022–2023

Building reach

60+ events and workshops. Telegram community of 4,000+. Temporary shelter opened for 70 families after the Kakhovka dam disaster. 78 tonnes of hygiene aid received from Avon. Beauty Days in 4 cities for 700+ women. International partnerships and NFT art exhibition at Web3 Stronger Together summit.

December 2023

Registered as an independent organization

Ukrainian Sisters incorporated as a standalone charitable organization, retaining our brand, community, strategy and focus exclusively on solo mothers and their children.

2024

Scale & system

50,000+ beneficiaries supported. Hubs active in Kyiv, Kharkiv and Kryvyi Rih. CRM, chatbot and digital referral system developed. Aid distributed in 10+ cities in partnership with regional collaborators.

August 2025 — Onward

Strategic phase 2025–2027

New strategic and operational plans approved. Focus shifts from crisis response toward empowerment, entrepreneurship, advocacy and systemic change — building the long-term model from survival to leadership.
