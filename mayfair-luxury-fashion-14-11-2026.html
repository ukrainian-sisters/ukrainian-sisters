On the runway this evening

Ukrainian Sisters: The Brand

Let tangible beauty become the foundation of every woman's wardrobe.

Our gloves join the fashion show - mesh and leather pieces produced in Kryvyi Rih, Ukraine, by a social enterprise that keeps working through the war. Every pair carries a name and a story: Жінка Жінці Дім, КОХАНА, СОНЯШНИК.

A few lucky guests will leave the evening with a pair as a gift.
